# Why I am puppy

Ok, so I'm puppy, what does that mean? If you want a super precise breakdown, please check out this [super cool study that was done](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/08164649.2025.2556256). But otherwise, I'm going to explain it from *my* point of view.

So first off, I'm a therian, if it wasn't obvious already. For as long as I can remember, I've always felt the sort of non-human vibes. It's been something that sat in my head before I even knew what furries were. I'm not sure when exactly I started to grivate towards wolves, but I know it was also before furry. When I learned what a furry was and asked what animal I'd be, I said wolf instantly. In all fairness, that does seem to be a lot of people's first choice.

I've come to realize that I'm actually closer to a wolf dog, no idea what all I'm made of, but it's something. On a spiritual level, I definitely feel it, but a huge reason why I say I'm puppy also comes from whatever level of neurodivergent I am.

## Puppy energy

My sense of puppy energy comes from a few places, but the first of which is literally that energy. I'm rarely exhausted, even on days which I only get around 2 hours of sleep, which is disturbingly often. Even on a good day, I can't seem to sleep more than 5-6 hours. I don't need caffeine to wake up, in fact, I can be up and about within moments of actually waking up. Caffeine does help keep my brain focused at least, which I believe is an ADHD trait.

ADHD is another part of it, some people would get mad at me for saying it, but puppy brain is a real thing for me. When it isn't distracting me from work, it's sort of validiating.

The strangest one comes from my illogical sense of trust. I think this is an autistic thing. I take everything said to me at face value. If a person says they're going to do something, I believe them, no matter how absurd it is. I do understand the difference, but I have to sort of talk to myself internally and make it clear when something is a joke or not. I'm also very trusting of people in general. I generally believe people are good and wouldn't try and harm me. But like a dog, I remain cautious and weary of new people trying to interact with my pack (which is either my friends, or people in SCG, dependind on the context) as opposed to me specifically.

I don't consider myself to be plural, but I am open to the idea of it. Sometimes I view my inner voice as two people, constantly having to reassess feelings and information to make sure we understand the reality of something.

Lastly, I'm wild. I enjoy being social, being around people, but equally enjoy my peace and solitude. I don't see myself as an introvert or extrovert, I am just comfortable. I'm really good at masking, and I'm really good at play time. I know whom I can be weird around, and whom I need to be "good" around. I feel like a very flexible person, which is probably just normal, but it feels different to me. I like being apart of things, and I like helping out where I can, I don't need treats or praise...but they're nice to have.

## Species affirmation stuff

- I wear a collar, almost all the time, I've gotten comfortable wearing it in public (contextually) and enjoy doing so. I feel safe with it on, and feel uncomfortable without it.
- I have puppy nip tattoos! 6 black dots, 3 on each side of my tummy. When I got them done, I literally presented it as a species affirmation tattoo and the artist who did it was super on board.
- I've been chipped! I'm on a registry for puppies! In case I get lost!
- I bark, I howl, I whine, I whimper, and I love the feeling of wagging my tail!
- I am clicker trained, and have a few hypno triggers that are all just puppy stuff. I bark on command, can no longer properly say the word "rough" and I'm ready to present my paws at a moments notice.
- I'm also trans (Wow, what a shocker)! I think this has been a huge influence on a lot of this. Getting in touch with my genuine self, wanting to present an image of myself that feels right. Taking off my human mask and shaking out my fur. It's all connected.
