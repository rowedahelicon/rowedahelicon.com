How could I have died if I wasn't alive?
I saw the world through a lens, surrounded by a face that never smiled.
I spoke with a voice that impersonated my soul.
Carried myself through a world I felt uncomfortable being seen in.
All under a name I never chose.

There was no one to kill, no one to die. Merely a shell, a prototype, a failed experiment of a man.
I stepped into the light, clawed my way out. Took over the controls. I became me.

This is my name, as I choose to be called it. This is my body, as I choose to present it.
This is my voice, as I choose to yield it.

I want to be seen, I want to be loved, I want to be alive.

I cry; not out of sadness, nor panic. I cry because I am happy; I cry became I am alive. I cry because despite all that has been given and all that has been taken, I want to live.

I am here.
